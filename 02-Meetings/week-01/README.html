Week 01 — Kickoff

Two meetings: launch the season, build the field, bond the team, first robot movement. Goals: every kid has driven the robot and every kid can name the six Core Values by the end of the week.

Meeting 1-A — Season launch (2 hrs)

Prep: season documents printed/open, kickoff video queued, SPIKE kits ready, engineering notebooks (or this folder) ready.

TimeActivity
0:00Welcome; coach intro — what FLL is, what the season looks like (keep to 5 min)
0:05Watch the season kickoff video together
0:20Core Values intro: the six values on poster paper — kids give examples of each
0:35Team identity: we're the Red Stormgears — design a logo/poster and agree how we introduce ourselves
0:50Break
1:00Build mission models — split bags among kids, build in parallel
1:45Field tour: place models on the mat; quick tour of each mission area
1:50Reflection + preview: next time we drive the robot!
2:00Clean-up (field stays set up if you have a permanent table)

Meeting 1-B — First drive (2 hrs)

TimeActivity
0:00Core Values game: pick from team-building-activities
0:10Stand-up: re-read meeting 1 notes; set today's goals
0:20Build/verify the SPIKE drive base (returning members lead, new members drive the build)
0:50Break
1:00First program: move forward 50 cm, stop. Then: can you hit a specific line?
1:30Mini-challenge: push a ball across the mat into a box — measure, adjust, retry
1:50Reflection; scribe logs "week 1" notebook entry
2:00Clean-up

Coach notes

  • Mission model bags are numbered — keep parts of a bag together until built.
  • Let returning members teach new members; coach stays out of the build.
  • If kit sorting is chaotic, spend 10 min on a parts-organizing system now — it pays back all season.
