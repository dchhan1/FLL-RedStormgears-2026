Where to find biodiversity-adjacent experts

  • University ecology / wildlife / environmental-science departments (look for

    • "outreach" or email a professor directly — many love kid questions)

  • Local & state parks: naturalists and interpretive rangers
  • Fish & wildlife agencies; county environmental / extension offices
  • Conservation nonprofits and land trusts (Audubon chapters, watershed groups)
  • Zoo / aquarium / science museum education departments
  • FIRST alumni / local FLL teams' mentors in science fields

Email template (kids should personalize every one)

Subject: Question from a FIRST LEGO League team studying biodiversity Dear Dr./Ms./Mr. ______, We are the Red Stormgears, a FIRST LEGO League team of 5th and 6th graders from ______. This season's theme is BIOGLOW, about biodiversity, and we are researching ______ (problem). We found your work on ______ and would love to ask you a few questions. Could we talk for 15–20 minutes — by video call, phone, or email, whatever is easiest for you? We are available ______. Thank you for considering it! The ______ team (kids' first names)

Tips: send to several people (reply rates are maybe 1 in 3) · mention the kids' ages (people say yes to kids) · always send a thank-you after, written by the kids. Start in week 3 — scheduling is the #1 project bottleneck.
