Interview question bank (kids pick 5–7)

  1. How did you get into this field?
  2. What's the biggest unsolved problem in your work?
  3. What do most people get wrong about it?
  4. If you had a tool that doesn't exist yet, what would it do?
  5. What would you try if money/time were no object?
  6. Who else should we talk to?
  7. Can we tell you our idea and get your honest opinion?

Source quality rules (teach these)

  • Prefer .edu / .gov / museums / journals; be careful with random blogs.
  • Cross-check surprising facts in a second source.
  • Write the date you accessed it.
