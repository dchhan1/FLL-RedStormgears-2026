Tournament

A typical event day: check-in, robot inspection, three robot rounds on the schedule, and three judging sessions — spread over 4–8 hours. The team's real job between sessions: stay calm, be Gracious Professionals, and have fun.

Coach mental model for the day

  1. The day is already won before you arrive. The robot runs and

    2. presentation are what they are; your job is to remove stress, not add it.

  2. Between-round fixes: one variable at a time, decided by the kids, with

    3. the pre-agreed fallback plan (run-strategy).

  3. Scoreboard volatility is normal. Rookie teams often see their best score

    4. in round 2 or 3 after nerves settle. Kids should know this in advance.

  4. Your team is being watched all day — pit behavior, practice-table

    5. etiquette, how they treat other teams. That's Core Values judging too.

  5. Whatever happens: the debrief is "what did we learn," never "what went wrong."
