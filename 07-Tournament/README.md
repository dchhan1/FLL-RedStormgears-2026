# Tournament

A typical event day: check-in, robot inspection, three robot rounds on the
schedule, and three judging sessions — spread over 4–8 hours. The team's real
job between sessions: stay calm, be Gracious Professionals, and have fun.

## Files

- [tournament-day-checklist.md](tournament-day-checklist.md) — the week before + day-of plan
- [packing-list.md](packing-list.md) — what goes in the bins

## Coach mental model for the day

1. **The day is already won before you arrive.** The robot runs and
   presentation are what they are; your job is to remove stress, not add it.
2. Between-round fixes: one variable at a time, decided by the kids, with
   the pre-agreed fallback plan ([run-strategy](../03-Robot-Game/run-strategy.md)).
3. Scoreboard volatility is normal. Rookie teams often see their best score
   in round 2 or 3 after nerves settle. Kids should know this in advance.
4. Your team is being watched all day — pit behavior, practice-table
   etiquette, how they treat other teams. That's Core Values judging too.
5. Whatever happens: the debrief is "what did we learn," never "what went wrong."
