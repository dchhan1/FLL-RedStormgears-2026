## Prioritization rules of thumb

1. **Rank missions by (points × success rate) ÷ time** — not raw points. A
   30-point mission you hit 2 times in 10 is worth less than a 15-pointer you
   hit 9 in 10.
2. Easy + high points first; a mission you can do 9/10 times is worth more
   than a harder one you hit 1/10 times.
3. Missions close to Launch Area before far ones — less travel, less error.
4. Group missions by area of the field so one run can attempt several.
5. Bonus/condition-based points (e.g., end-of-match states) are often cheap — check them.
