Robot Lessons — Skill Ladder

Teach in this order. Each lesson: 10 min concept → 25 min hands-on challenge → 5 min debrief. New kids climb the ladder; returning kids re-verify skills and teach them (teaching is the best retention).

Coaching the ladder

  • Challenge-first, instruction-second: pose the challenge, let them struggle a

    • bit, teach the concept when they hit the wall.

  • A skill is "done" when the kids can use it in a real mission run, not just

    • the lesson exercise.

  • Returning members: assign each one a lesson to teach a newer member. Prep

    • them the meeting before.

  • These lessons deliberately mirror the free PrimeLessons.org Word Blocks

    • curriculum (10 units, ending in FLL-specific debugging/reliability) — pull their slides when you want fuller explanations or a Python track.
