Lesson 05 — Attachments

Concepts: passive vs. powered; the quick-swap philosophy. Matches are 2.5 minutes — time lost fiddling with pins is points lost.

Warm-up (5 min)

Hand them a mission needing "lift and carry." Ask for three ideas in five minutes, sketch each. No building yet.

Teach (10 min)

  • Passive: no motors — pushers, hooks, guides, dumps. Simple, light,

    • reliable. Build these first.

  • Powered: uses a motor — lifts, grabs, spins. Uses one of your limited

    • motor ports and adds failure modes.

  • Quick-swap: one consistent mount (e.g., one standard hole pattern or a

    • single pin pair) so the copilot can swap in <10 seconds. Design the system once, then every attachment snaps into it.

  • Keep the robot's center of gravity low; heavy top attachments tip on turns.

Hands-on (25 min)

  1. Design and build the team's standard attachment mount on the drive base.
  2. Build one passive attachment for the team's current easiest mission.
  3. Time the swap: copilot swaps in under 15 seconds. Practice it like a sport.

Debrief (5 min)

  • Where do attachments wobble? What braces them?
  • Photo → design evolution log, v-next.

Success criteria

Standard mount built and documented One passive attachment used successfully in a real mission run Swap drill under 15 seconds
