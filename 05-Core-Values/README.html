Core Values

FLL's six Core Values are judged directly at tournaments and shape every meeting. We do a 10-minute activity at the start of every session.

The six values (this season's official wording may vary slightly — check the season documents)

ValueIn practice for a 5th–6th grade team
DiscoveryWe explore new skills and ideas — trying and failing counts
InnovationWe use creativity and persistence to solve problems
ImpactWe apply what we learn to improve our world
InclusionWe respect each other and embrace our differences
TeamworkWe are stronger when we work together
FunWe enjoy and celebrate what we do!

Alongside these, FIRST's philosophies of Gracious Professionalism and Coopertition (competing while helping others) run through everything.

Coach notes

  • Judges often watch teams at the practice table and in the pit, not just in

    • the Core Values session — the values have to be real, not rehearsed.

  • At judging, expect a hands-on team challenge on the spot: they'll be given a

    • surprise task and watched on how they work together, plus how they talk about their season. Practice mystery challenges (activities #7, #8).

  • When conflicts arise at meetings, refer to the team's own written norms

    • rather than imposing a ruling.
