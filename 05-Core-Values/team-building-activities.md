# Team-Building Activities — 10–15 min openers

## 1. Mirror Build (Teamwork, Inclusion)

Pairs sit back-to-back. One builds a small LEGO shape; then explains it
verbally so the partner builds the same thing. Compare. Rotate roles.
**Debrief:** What words worked? What was frustrating?

## 2. Silent Line-Up (Inclusion, Teamwork)

Line up by birthday — no talking, no writing. Then by first name alphabetically.
**Debrief:** How did you communicate? Who got left out? How did that feel?

## 3. Tower Challenge (Teamwork, Innovation)

Build the tallest free-standing tower from 30 pieces in 8 minutes. Constraint
round: one hand behind each back, or only one kid may touch the pieces and the
others direct. **Debrief:** What roles emerged? What constraint was hardest?

## 4. What Went Well / Even Better If (Discovery, Fun)

Closing round, every meeting: each kid says one thing that worked and one
thing to change. No elaboration allowed — keep it snappy. Log in
[reflections.md](reflections.md).

## 5. Blindfold Navigation (Teamwork, Trust)

One kid blindfolded; teammates guide them across the room to a target using
only words. (Coach: safety spot.) **Debrief:** Clear instructions are hard —
that's why we test our assumptions.

## 6. Gracious Professionalism Scenarios (all values)

Read a scenario; team discusses what a Gracious Professional would do:
- Another team's robot breaks before their round and they're panicking.
- A teammate keeps taking over the programming and won't share the laptop.
- You see another team's run sheet lying on a table before your round.
- Your robot fails at the tournament and your teammate starts crying.
**Debrief:** What would you actually do — not just the "right answer"?

## 7. Mystery Challenge (Teamwork) — *this one mimics judging*

Give the team a surprise 10-minute task with limited materials: "build a
bridge between two chairs that holds this book," "sort these mixed parts into
sensible categories and explain your system." Watch how they organize; debrief
roles. Do this monthly — the judged Core Values task is exactly this shape.

## 8. Role Shuffle (Inclusion, Discovery)

Mid-season meeting where every kid swaps to their least-favorite job for the
session: the programmer builds, the builder documents. **Debrief:** What did
you learn about your teammates' work?

## 9. Compliment Tag (Fun, Impact)

Each kid names a specific thing another member did well this season, then
"tags" them to go next until everyone has been tagged.

## 10. Estimation Game (Fun, Discovery)

Quick guesses before measuring: "How many seconds will this run take?"
"How many points is our best round?" Closest guess wins bragging rights.

## Rotation suggestion (2 meetings/week)

- Meeting A: #4 (reflection) or a quick game (#2, #10)
- Meeting B: one substantive activity (#1, #3, #6, #7, #8)
- Once a month: #7 Mystery Challenge
