Notes on using this calendar

  • If something slips, cut robot missions — never cut presentation rehearsal.

    • Judging sessions (Robot Design, Innovation Project, Core Values) carry as much weight as robot score at most events, and rookie teams win awards through them.

  • With 4 kids on a small team, everyone touches everything. Rotate "roles of

    • the week": programmer, documenter, photographer, project lead — so every kid can speak to all parts in judging.

  • Keep meetings at 2 hours: 10 min opening/team building → 90 min work block →

    • 10 min reflection & documentation. End with the notebook, always.
